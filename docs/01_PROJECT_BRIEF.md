# Project Brief

## Identity

- English working name: Cat Guard: Tower Defense.
- Russian working name: КотоОборона: башни и хвосты.
- Platform priority: Android-only first.
- Business model: free-to-play.
- First business goal: earn the first money from a small mobile game.

## Product Target

- Target income after 6 months: 100-500 USD per month.
- Genre: 2D tower defense / merge defense / RPG-lite.
- Session length: 5-10 minutes.
- MVP size: 10-20 levels.
- Monetization: rewarded ads later, without intrusive ads.

## First Version Principles

- Keep scope small enough to finish.
- Prefer simple readable systems over clever abstractions.
- Validate fun and retention before adding monetization.
- Avoid paid assets, server features, multiplayer, and iOS until separate tasks exist.
