## English Full Description

```text
Cat Guard: Tower Defense is a landscape strategy game about a squad of guardian cats defending cozy garden paths from waves of pests.

Build a defense from five distinct tower families, choose upgrades during battle, change targeting priorities, and charge Guardian ultimates through play. Follow enemy routes across larger maps, adapt to special rules, and face multi-phase bosses before they reach the goal.

Expansion candidate content:
- 12 campaign maps across three garden biomes
- Five guardian tower families with branching battle upgrades
- Multi-route paths, challenge rules, mini-bosses, and the Rat King finale
- Three Guardian ultimates with earned charge and map-scale effects
- Garden Outpost home hub, quests, contracts, achievements, and progression
- Pause, 1x/2x speed, text scale, reduced-flash, and camera-shake settings
- Russian and English interface text and tutorials
- Offline-friendly play with local device saves

No account or cloud connection is required in the current build. Progress stays on the device and can be removed through the in-game reset action, Android app-data settings, or uninstalling.

This candidate is entering closed testing. Feedback about difficulty, readability, touch controls, performance, accessibility comfort, and first-session clarity is especially useful.
```

## Store Listing Guardrails

- Do not claim rankings, awards, price promotions, download counts, or production readiness.
- Do not claim live ads, analytics, purchases, cloud saves, accounts, or backend services while those SDKs are absent.
- Screenshots must come from the non-development `0.2.0` candidate and must not contain synthetic UI, device frames, a Unity splash, or a `Development Build` watermark.
- If screenshots contain localized UI text, upload matching RU and EN sets where practical.
- Final developer identity, support/privacy contact, privacy-policy URL, audience, and content-rating answers remain owner inputs.
